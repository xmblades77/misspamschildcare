About Me

Meet Miss Pam

Miss Pam
Owner & Lead Caregiver
Licensed Provider #100406

I'm a reliable mother of two sons, and it's my own family that led me here. What started as a mom working for the Board of Education wanting to stay home with her own children turned into an important career enriching the lives of other children and their families.

I believe family child care matters because it keeps kids in a home-like atmosphere while giving them a safe, nurturing place to explore, learn, and grow — with more individualized attention than a larger center can offer.

"I commit myself to providing quality childcare with structure, dependability, and consistency — I'll always continue to strive to give your child the best care possible."

Children in my care get to learn in a creative, playful, and genuinely fun environment, and above all, they're encouraged to discover and express their own uniqueness, interests, and talents.
